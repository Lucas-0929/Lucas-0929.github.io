About the Service

Campus Helper is a student-run errand service on campus. We pick up parcels, buy snacks and print documents, so you can save time between classes.

Rules

The service works only inside the campus. Orders are handled by student runners, and same-day delivery is available when the time slot is open.

FAQ

Q: How long does delivery take?
A: Usually within one hour after the runner accepts your order.

Q: Can I order on weekends?
A: Yes, we are open from 10:00 to 20:00 on weekends.
